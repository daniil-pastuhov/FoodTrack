BestBefore is an expiry date tracker and food inventory for your fridge, freezer and pantry: scan groceries, see what to use first, get a reminder before food goes off, and turn what's about to expire into a recipe.

<b>Add food in seconds</b>
• Scan a barcode and the product name and picture are filled in for you (via Open Food Facts)
• Point the camera at the printed date and BestBefore reads the expiry date for you
• Snap a photo of fruit or vegetables and it suggests what it is
• Batch mode: scan a whole bag of shopping in one go
• Read a shop receipt to add several items at once

<b>Never miss a date</b>
• Works with best-before and use-by dates
• Reminders a few days before something expires, at the time you choose
• "Expiring Soon" and "Expired" lists at a glance
• Home-screen widgets for expiring items, your shopping list and a quick overview

<b>Shop smarter</b>
• Essentials you use up go straight onto your shopping list
• Share the list as text or a QR code
• Optional: get your shopping list when you arrive at a shop you've saved

<b>Cook what's expiring</b>
• The Recipe tab picks the items closest to their date; change the selection, servings, time, diet and more
• Free: copy or share a ready-made prompt to the AI app you already use
• Free on supported Android phones: the recipe is written on the phone itself, by its own model
• Recipe packs (in-app purchase): recipes written by our server and saved in the app, and a 3-day meal plan ("Menu for 3 days") that uses what expires first

<b>Your data</b>
• No ads, no tracking
• No subscription: the app is free, and recipe packs are optional one-off purchases
• Your pantry is stored on your phone; export a backup whenever you like
• No account needed, not even to buy a pack. Sign in with Google only if you want your recipes on other phones
• A recipe from a pack sends the items you tick, your recipe settings and your allergens to our server. The free ways send nothing to us
• Available in English, Russian and Norwegian

Waste less food, save money and always know what you have at home.
