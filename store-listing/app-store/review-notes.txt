1. NEW IN THIS VERSION
The Calendar tab is replaced by a Recipe tab. It adds the app's first in-app purchases (three consumable recipe packs, submitted with this version) and an optional sign-in. Everything else works as before, with no account.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
BestBefore helps households waste less food. It lists the food at home with expiry dates, reminds the user a few days before something expires and suggests a recipe for what needs using first. Audience: adults managing a household. The app is free, with no ads or tracking; the pantry stays on the device.

3. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No login, demo account or sample files are needed.
- Add food: with the scan button, scan a food barcode, then the printed date (or pick a date). "+" adds an item by hand.
- Home: all food, sorted by expiry.
- Shopping List: essentials move here when used up.
- Settings: reminders, and optional "Remind me near a shop" (with "Always" location access, the shopping list shows on arrival at a saved shop; location never leaves the device).
- Recipe: writes a recipe from the items closest to their date. Free: "Share prompt" hands a text prompt to any app; with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later) "Generate on this iPhone" writes it on device. Paid: packs of 10, 50 or 100 recipes; one recipe is one dish from our server. "Menu set" (1 to 4 days, 1 to 3 courses a day) is server-only and takes several recipes, as the form shows. "Add to shopping list" adds its missing ingredients. "Pictures" (single pack recipes): the dish, or also tricky steps, labelled as made by AI, for the extra recipes shown. "Report this recipe" ends every result from our server (reason, optional note).

TO TEST THE PURCHASE (sandbox purchases work during review, within a daily limit shared by all sandbox testers):
1. Add an item or two.
2. Open the Recipe tab and tap "Buy recipes".
3. Choose "On this iPhone, no account", or sign in with Apple or Google.
4. Buy the 10-recipe pack. The sheet says that on a refund request we tell Apple how many recipes were used. The balance shows 10. If it says the purchase is being confirmed, tap "Try now".
5. Tap "Generate recipe". If allergens are set in Settings, the app first asks consent to send them. The recipe appears in seconds; the balance drops to 9.
Deletion: Recipe tab > Recipe settings > Account > Delete account (signed in), or Your recipes > "Delete my data from our server" (bought without an account).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org): on a barcode scan the app fetches the product's name, picture and nutrition data, sending only the barcode number.
- Our server (Firebase Cloud Functions, Auth, App Check), reached only once a purchase is started: it verifies the purchase with Apple and, for a paid recipe, sends the ticked item names (all if "Send all products" is on), the staples' names (Settings > Staples), the recipe settings and the allergen list (only after explicit consent) to Google Gemini (Vertex AI) and returns the recipe. Pictures: Google's image model (Vertex AI) draws them from a dish description our server writes; no user text is copied in.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, both optional. Payments are Apple in-app purchases only.
- Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. No analytics.
- On the device: AVFoundation, Vision, Core Location region monitoring, UserNotifications, WidgetKit, SwiftData, StoreKit, Foundation Models.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Same in all regions and in English, Russian, Norwegian, Spanish, German, French, Swedish and Danish. Open Food Facts covers some countries better; an unknown barcode's name is typed in.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry. Recipes are cooking suggestions, not medical or dietary advice; the app tells users to check labels for allergens. Third-party material: product data and pictures from Open Food Facts (database ODbL, pictures CC BY-SA; https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use).
