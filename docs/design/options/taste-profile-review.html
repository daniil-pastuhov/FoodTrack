Taste profile

Design for review, 15 Nov 2026. Every new screen, light and dark. Self-contained: Vollkorn falls back to Georgia here; the design file uses the real font. The full set, with triggers and the tablet layout, is the Taste profile page of design.html.

Decisions to confirm

  1. 16 Nov: a question added after saving shows as "· 1 new question" in accent green at the end of the row's line, and the quiz opens at that step with Save in the top bar. Dropped answers are never announced.
  2. 16 Nov: a dot on the Recipe header's settings button (and on the row of someone who never opened the quiz) while there are questions not seen yet; it matches the Saved recipes count badge.
  3. 16 Nov: the quiz content comes from our server and is drawn by kind; the first open without a cache shows a loader, and a failed load shows Retry and Close. Copy no longer promises a number of questions.
  4. The row sits on its own between Language and Your recipes (not inside the account card, whose content changes in five states).
  5. Skip and Next share one slot: Skip (outline) while the step is empty, Next (filled) once something is chosen.
  6. At the cap the other chips dim and the helper says "That's 6. Untick one to choose another."; nothing is ever replaced silently.
  7. Heat is three radio cards (Mild, Medium, Hot with one to three flames); tapping the chosen one again clears it.
  8. Favourites are grouped under Vegetables and beans, Protein and dairy, Fruit; the other lists are plain chips.
  9. Love vs skip: a chip ticked in the other list shows a small mark; choosing it moves it, with a T3 snackbar and Undo. Proposed: extend the rule to flavour Garlic vs skip Garlic.
  10. Leaving midway asks only if an answer changed (the app's existing Discard changes dialog).
  11. While editing, Save is also in the top bar on every step.
  12. Clear taste profile lives at the foot of step 1 when a profile exists; it clears at once, with Undo.
  13. After Save: back to Recipe settings, the row shows the summary, T3 snackbar. No summary screen.
  14. The promo sheet becomes a title, a lede and three short rows (proposed in place of the spec's one paragraph).
