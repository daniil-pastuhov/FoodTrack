Taste profile

Added 15 Nov 2026. Six short, skippable questions in Recipe settings (cuisines, heat, flavours, what you love, what you'd rather skip, how you cook), fixed choices only, kept on the device and sent only with recipe requests from a pack, so the server writes to the person's taste. Shown only where packs are offered; without recipes in a pack, a short sheet says what it does. Decisions are in brief.md (2026-11-15) and the owner's review page is options/taste-profile-review.html.

16 Nov 2026: the quiz is the server's content. The steps, their order and icons, the questions, options, caps, grouping and group titles, the heat cards' subtitles and flame levels, and the note under a question (such as the allergy line) come from our server (getTasteQuiz), cached per language. The apps draw them by kind: multi as chips, optionally under group titles; single as radio cards with a subtitle and one to three flames; tone: avoid with the ban sign and the "rather skip" wording; the step icon by name, with a neutral fallback (utensils) for a name the app doesn't have. The six steps drawn here are the initial content, not a fixed list; see the seventh-step and long-list states. The apps bundle only the chrome: titles, progress, actions, helpers, snackbars, the promo sheet, loading and error.

Taste profile

Recipes from your packs, written the way you like to eat.

  • A few short questions
    Cuisines, heat, flavours, what you love, what you'd rather skip and how you like to cook. Skip any of them.
  • Recipes and menus to your taste
    Our server leans towards what you like and leaves out what you'd rather skip. Your pantry, allergens and diet still come first.
  • Kept on this device
    It is sent only with requests for recipes from a pack.

Geschmacksprofil

Rezepte aus deinen Paketen, so geschrieben, wie du gern isst.

  • Ein paar kurze Fragen
    Küchen, Schärfe, Aromen, was du liebst, was du lieber weglässt und wie du gern kochst. Jede Frage kannst du überspringen.
  • Rezepte und Menüs nach deinem Geschmack
    Unser Server richtet sich nach deinen Vorlieben und lässt weg, was du lieber nicht isst. Deine Vorräte, Allergene und deine Ernährungsweise haben weiterhin Vorrang.
  • Bleibt auf diesem Gerät
    Es wird nur mit Anfragen nach Rezepten aus einem Paket gesendet.
