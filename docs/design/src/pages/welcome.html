Free recipes for signing in

Added 16 Nov 2026. Someone who signs in gets a few recipes from our server for free, once per Apple or Google account, so they can try what a pack gives. How many is our server's answer (getWelcomeOffer, asked at most once a day when the Recipe tab is shown, sending nothing about the person; welcomeRecipes beside the balance once signed in). The offer always names the real number ("Sign in and get 3 free recipes"); with no answer above zero every place stays exactly as today. Free recipes then behave exactly like recipes from a pack, taste profile included. Drawn with the current form: the action group at the end of the form (2026-11-15) and menu sets. Each place is drawn with an offer of 3, of 1, and none. Decisions are in brief.md (2026-11-16); the owner's review page is options/welcome-recipes-review.html.
