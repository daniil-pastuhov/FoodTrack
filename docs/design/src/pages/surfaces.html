Widgets and notifications

The current titles conflict with the no-alarm brief, and the bodies break the no-exclamation rule. Russian and Norwegian are drafts for translator review. Title and body keys also exist in iOS Localizable.strings, so changing them touches six files. Fallback and channel strings are Android only. Plural forms are shown once.
