Pictures, and reporting a recipe

17 Nov 2026. Decided by the owner today: the person chooses between no pictures, one picture of the finished dish, or the dish plus pictures of the steps that are hard to follow from words alone; the choice starts at no pictures and is remembered; every result our server wrote can be reported with a reason, and our server stores the reports and tells the owner; a recipe written by the phone's own model has no report action and nothing about it is ever sent (owner, 2026-11-17). Still proposals (brief.md): the costs (2 and 3 sparks), no pictures for menus, the not-enough behaviour, the give-back rule and everything else on this page. Pictures are for a single recipe written by our server only, never the shared prompt or the phone's own model. Each level's cost is our server's (costs.recipeImage, costs.recipeImageSteps), never hard-coded; a level our server doesn't price, or a server without acceptsRecipeImage, is simply not offered. The pictures are made in the same request as the recipe, after it passed our checks, from short descriptions the recipe model writes; nothing the person typed goes into a picture's prompt. Picture areas are stand-ins (a tinted block with an icon): no external images on these pages.

Spinach and yoghurt eggs on sourdough toast

A warm, quick plate that uses the bread, the spinach and one of the yoghurts today.

15 min2 servingsEasy
Past its date: Sourdough bread (1 day). Check it is still safe to eat before you cook.

Ingredients

  • 4 slicesSourdough breadIn your pantry
  • 100 gBaby spinachIn your pantry
  • 150 gGreek yoghurtIn your pantry
  • 4Free-range eggsIn your pantry
  • 1 cloveGarlic
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 1 pinchChilli flakes
  • Salt and pepper

Ingredients without the “In your pantry” mark aren't from your pantry: staples, or a few things you may need to buy.

Method

Step pictures are made by AI. Yours may look different.

  1. 1

    Stir the grated garlic and a pinch of salt into the yoghurt and leave it at room temperature.

  2. 2

    Toast the bread. Day-old sourdough toasts better than fresh.

  3. 3

    Warm the oil in a pan, add the spinach and turn it for a minute until it wilts. Season.

  4. 4

    Fry or poach the eggs to your liking.

  5. 5

    Spread the yoghurt on the toast, pile on the spinach, top with the eggs and finish with chilli flakes.

TipCheck the bread before using it: one day past a best-before date is usually fine, but not if there is any mould.

Spinach and yoghurt eggs on sourdough toast

A warm, quick plate that uses the bread, the spinach and one of the yoghurts today.

15 min2 servingsEasy
Past its date: Sourdough bread (1 day). Check it is still safe to eat before you cook.

Ingredients

  • 4 slicesSourdough breadIn your pantry
  • 100 gBaby spinachIn your pantry
  • 150 gGreek yoghurtIn your pantry
  • 4Free-range eggsIn your pantry
  • 1 cloveGarlic
  • 1 tbspOlive oil
  • 1 pinchChilli flakes
  • Salt and pepper

Ingredients without the “In your pantry” mark aren't from your pantry: staples, or a few things you may need to buy.

Method

Step pictures are made by AI. Yours may look different.

  1. 1

    Stir the grated garlic and a pinch of salt into the yoghurt and leave it at room temperature.

  2. 2

    Toast the bread. Day-old sourdough toasts better than fresh.

  3. 3

    Warm the oil in a pan, add the spinach and turn it for a minute until it wilts. Season.

  4. 4

    Fry or poach the eggs to your liking.

  5. 5

    Spread the yoghurt on the toast, pile on the spinach, top with the eggs and finish with chilli flakes.
