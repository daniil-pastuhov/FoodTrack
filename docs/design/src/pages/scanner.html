Scanner

Full screen over the camera, with no tab bar. One bar at the bottom picks how to capture: Barcode, Batch, Receipt, Date or Photo. Close, the torch and Type it in stay on screen, so there is always a way out that doesn't need the camera.

The camera stays dark

The picture, its controls and the mode bar are dark in both themes, because a bright interface around a live picture dazzles and hides what the camera sees. They use the dark values of the normal roles, so nothing new enters the palette. Result panels and dialogs follow the theme.

The picture can be any brightness, so text on it always sits on the scrim at 72%. With a white packet behind it, on-surface still reaches 6.3:1. On-surface-variant only reaches 4.1:1 there, so it isn't used on the picture. The aiming frame has a light ring and a dark ring, so one of them always clears 3:1. Pictures on this page are drawn stand-ins.
