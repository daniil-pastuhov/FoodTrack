## How to join (one page for testers)

**BestBefore test: how to join** (Android phone only; about five minutes)

1. On your phone, open the **Play Store**, tap your profile picture at the top right and note
   which Google account is shown. Use that same account below.
2. Open this link and tap **Join group**: {{GROUP_URL}}
   (If you're asked to sign in, sign in with the account from step 1.)
3. Open this link: {{OPT_IN_URL}}
   Tap **Become a tester**. The page then says you are a tester.
   If it says the app is not available, wait a few minutes and try again; Google can take a
   while after you join the group.
4. On the same page, tap **Download it on Google Play**, then **Install**.
   It shows up as "BestBefore" (or "Matvakt" / "Срокоед" depending on your phone's language).
5. Open the app once and look around: add something from your kitchen.
6. **Don't leave the test**, and keep the app installed for at least three weeks. Leaving and
   joining again starts the count over.

Good to know:

- It's free. There are no ads and you don't need to create an account.
- Your food list is stored only on your phone. Crash reports are sent so I can fix bugs. Only
  if you use a recipe pack are the items you pick (with your recipe settings and allergies) sent
  to the server that writes the recipe.
- The Recipe tab can sell recipe packs. As a tester you would pay real money for them, so
  don't buy unless you want to. If you'd like to try buying without paying, ask me to add you as
  a test buyer.
- Feedback: {{FEEDBACK}}, or in the Play Store on the app's page ("Send private feedback").
- To leave after the test: open {{OPT_IN_URL}} and tap **Leave the program**.
